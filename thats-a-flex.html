That’s A Flex.

Launching Zip Plus by turning financial flexibility into something people could instantly get.

Zip Co.Launch strategy & campaign leadership

Show your money who’s boss.

Money Your Way gave Zip a broader brand promise. The Zip Plus launch had to make that promise tangible: explain the product quickly and give people a reason to care.

I led the launch across agency, creative and media. That’s A Flex brought the promise to life through direct, benefit-led lines: ‘Lose interest in paying interest’ and ‘A credit limit. Not a credit card.’ Outdoor, video and social established the idea; shorter paid executions and creator content made it work in the feed.

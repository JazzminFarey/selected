Heart Foundation AustraliaMarketing strategy, brand & product development

Each and every heart.

The idea was to give people something they would want to wear or use, with a message worth sharing. The Each and Every Heart range put women’s heart health on clothing and everyday objects, with proceeds supporting Heart Foundation research.

My work connected the brand and product range with the shopping experience. A Shopify store, paid and organic promotion, and a planned Salesforce Marketing Cloud journey took people from discovering the range to buying and staying involved.

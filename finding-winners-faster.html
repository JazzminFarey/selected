The principle

Make the next brief better.

A creative system should help a team make decisions. Start with the audience, proposition and commercial objective, then be clear about what the work needs to achieve and what you want to learn.

How I think about the system
  1. 01

    Agree the job. Who is this for, and what should change?

  2. 02

    Give the idea room. Keep the proposition clear across executions.

  3. 03

    Read the response in context. Attention, action and customer value matter together.

  4. 04

    Carry the learning into the next brief, creative decision and investment.

The commercial connection

Creative decisions should connect to business decisions.

Performance thinking helps decide what to develop, adapt or stop. Brand judgment keeps those decisions connected to a recognisable, coherent idea over time.

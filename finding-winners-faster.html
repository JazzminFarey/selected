Finding Winners Faster.

Turning creative performance into a better next brief, and better decisions about where to invest.

Zip Co.Performance creative & acquisition leadership

More creative should mean more learning.

We were producing more assets, but the learning was getting lost at the level of individual ads. I changed the approach so we could see what the work had in common: the hook, the subject, the format and the message.

We grouped performance across those elements, read attention alongside leads and acquisition cost, and used the patterns to brief the next round. The point was to build on what worked, rather than start every brief from scratch.

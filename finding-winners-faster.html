System

From launch and wait to iterate properly.

We were producing more creative, but learning less from it. PMax was always on, feeding signal. We changed how we read performance. Creative was broken into parts, hook length, subject, stats vs no stats. An AI agent grouped performance across those elements, so we could see patterns across everything running, not just individual ads.

  1. 01

    Break creative into parts. Hook length, subject, stats vs no stats.

  2. 02

    Group performance across those parts with an AI agent.

  3. 03

    Read CTR for attention. Read leads and CPA for what converted.

  4. 04

    Start new creative from what was already working, not from scratch.

Signal

We proved what actually moved the number, before scaling it.

Speed of testing became the advantage. The same thinking carried into how we spoke to existing customers, shifting from broad segmentation to where marketing actually changed behaviour.

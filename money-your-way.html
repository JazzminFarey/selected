Money Your Way.

Everything we tested sounded like every other financial product. Category-shaped language was costing us comprehension at the top of the funnel, and conversion underneath it. The shift wasn’t louder. It was different.

Hooks

    Money that doesn’t talk like a bank.

The Shift

Stop sounding like the category.

A simple, repeatable message, one with personality the category doesn’t have, adapted for paid not just brand. Same line, different cuts. Tested across feeds, search and out of home.

What we made
  • One repeatable line, many cuts.
  • Built for paid, not just brand.
  • Hook-first variants tested by feed and audience.
Signal

Different language meant faster understanding, and stronger conversion.

Money Your Way.

Taking Zip beyond buy now, pay later. A brand platform built around money on your terms.

Zip Co.Brand strategy & campaign leadership

Lead with what people want: control.

Zip was expanding into a broader range of credit products, but people still knew it for one thing: buy now, pay later. The brand needed to make room for where the business was going.

I developed Money Your Way around a simple truth: traditional credit feels rigid; Zip offers flexibility. ‘Money’ placed us in the wider financial category. ‘Your Way’ put the customer in control. I led the platform’s rollout across campaigns, product messaging, CRM, merchant channels and PR.

One idea, from brand to product.

That’s A Flex became the first product expression for Zip Plus. The same promise of flexibility could build the brand and give people a clear reason to consider a product.

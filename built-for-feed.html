Built for Feed.

Creator-led ads made for the way people actually watch, scroll and shop.

Zip Co.Creator strategy & performance creative

Stop competing with the feed. Become it.

Our ads were being skipped before they had a chance to explain the product. We changed how we made them: a person, a clear hook and a reason to keep watching.

I led the team shaping creator briefs and performance creative. Each brief had a clear product message, with room for the creator’s own voice. We tested different openings, angles and personalities, then used what held attention and converted to shape the next round.

Built for Feed.

Our ads were being skipped before they even started. So we rebuilt for the feed itself, face-first, hook-first, creator-led, and ran high volumes of variations across angles and personalities to find what held.

The Shift

Build content that feels native to the feed.

Stop competing with the feed. Become it. Briefs gave creators clear hooks and freedom on tone, then we let signal pick the winners and brief the next round from there.

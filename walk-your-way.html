Walk Your Way.

A paid social campaign showing that a walking plan can fit the life you already lead.

OrganisationHeart Foundation Australia
FocusCreative + Acquisition
My contributionLed strategy, analysis, creative and stakeholder management.
Creative creditHeart Foundation team.

The idea

Make the first step feel easy.

The 2022 campaign promoted the Heart Foundation’s six-week Personalised Walking Plan. Bright imagery showed walking alone, with friends and outdoors, making the product feel flexible and achievable.

My contribution

Creative informed by channel learning.

I led the campaign strategy, creative, analysis and stakeholder management. Prior learning informed an always-on Facebook approach, concentrating acquisition investment in a single channel.

Reported campaign results

Reported acquisition cost against a $9 KPI.

Barnardos Buddies.

A regular-giving product that makes the donor’s connection to a child’s journey more tangible.

OrganisationBarnardos Australia
FocusProduct + Identity
My contributionLed creative direction, product development and rollout.
Creative creditAlana Indratheb

The idea

Make a lasting connection.

Barnardos Buddies invited supporters to follow a child’s progress through personalised communications and digital experiences. Illustrated characters gave the product a recognisable identity while protecting children’s identities.

My contribution

Product + Identity

I led the creative direction, product development and rollout across paid social, outdoor, telemarketing and face-to-face acquisition. The donor experience extended beyond sign-up through progress updates, activity books and opportunities to interact.

Jump Rope for Heart.

An illustrated adventure that brought a school fundraising program into the classroom.

The idea

A story worth opening.

Happy Heart travels around Australia looking for the perfect skipping companions, before discovering they were children all along. The eight-page storybook invited teachers and students into the program, then unfolded into an A3 poster about the benefits of skipping.

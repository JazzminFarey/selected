BeliefsSix things I believe.

01 / 06
  1. 01

    The hook does the work. If it does not hold in three seconds, nothing else matters.

  2. 02

    Variation beats a perfect ad. Test the variable, not the asset.

  3. 03

    Read across signals. CTR, hold rate, CVR and CAC never tell the full story alone.

  4. 04

    The winning ad is a system, not an accident.

  5. 05

    Speed of iteration beats volume of output. Cut what is not working faster.

  6. 06

    AI is useful for variation. Human judgement decides what is worth keeping.

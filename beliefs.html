01. Ideas

Start with the audience, the proposition and a point of view. A good brief gives the team clarity about the problem and room to find an interesting answer.

02. Systems

Build a recognisable idea that can work across a campaign, a product story and a social post. Give teams enough structure to stay coherent and enough freedom to make good work.

03. Economics

Know what the work is there to change. Read attention, customer behaviour and commercial value together, then use that understanding to make better decisions.

BeliefsSix things I believe.

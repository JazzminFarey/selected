What it does

AI visibility for brands. Where you show up, where you drop out, what shifts it.

Better. shows whether your brand is actually being recommended by AI, where it drops out of the answer, and which signals influence the next recommendation.

Not another dashboard. A way to see if you show up when it matters, and a plan to do something about it.

What we look atSignals that move AI recommendations.

04
01. AI visibility

Are you in the answer? Across the prompts your buyer is actually using, in the models they actually use.

02. Recommendation gaps

Where you should appear and don’t. Which competitors are taking the slot, and what they’re saying that you aren’t.

03. Authority signals

Mentions, citations, third-party proof, structured content, expert presence. The trust the model is reading, not just the marketing you’re publishing.

04. Content execution

The pages, posts and proof points the model can actually see. Built to be cited, not just clicked.

The output

A 90-day action plan.

  • Diagnostic of where you appear, where you don’t, and why.
  • Prioritised gaps, ranked by commercial impact and effort.
  • Content, proof and authority moves to make in the next 90 days.
  • Re-measurement at day 30, 60 and 90.

Same thinking as creative. Stay close to the signal. Push what works. Cut what does not.

Less about being visible everywhere. More about being chosen.

Better.

How does a brand become the one people are recommended? I’m building Better. to explore that question as AI becomes part of how people discover and choose.

What it does

Connecting brand choice with AI recommendations.

Better. is being built to show whether a brand appears in relevant AI recommendations, where it is absent, and what content and proof may help address those gaps.

The question brings together positioning, customer intent and execution: what makes a brand relevant at the moment someone is choosing?

What we look atQuestions shaping the product.

AI visibility

Are you in the answer? Across questions relevant to the category and the buying decision.

Recommendation gaps

Where you should appear and don’t. Which competitors are taking the slot, and what they’re saying that you aren’t.

Authority signals

Mentions, citations, third-party proof, structured content, expert presence. Evidence that can support a brand’s claims beyond its own marketing.

Content execution

The pages, posts and proof points the model can actually see. Clear enough to understand and useful enough to support a choice.

The output

From recommendation gaps to action.

  • Understand where a brand appears and where it is absent.
  • Prioritise gaps by relevance to the buying decision.
  • Identify content, positioning and proof worth developing.
  • Revisit recommendations over time to understand what changes.

That is the direction I’m building towards: connecting recommendation intelligence with practical marketing decisions and creative execution.

Less about being visible everywhere. More about being chosen.

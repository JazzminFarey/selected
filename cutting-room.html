The Cutting Room.

Knowing what to leave out is part of making good work. A few principles I use to question an idea before asking a team to take it further.

These are general reflections on creative decisions, rather than an archive of unpublished client or employer work.

FormatNotes
Items06
CategoriesIdeas, Brand, Proposition, Craft
Relevance

Give the idea a job.

An interesting execution needs a clear connection to the audience, the proposition and the problem.

01 / Idea
Clarity

Find the thought underneath.

If an idea takes a long explanation, I want to know whether the thought is unclear or the execution is getting in its way.

02 / Proposition
Recognition

Make it belong to the brand.

The work should carry a recognisable point of view, even when the format or creator changes.

03 / Brand
Focus

Let the strongest idea lead.

Trying to say everything can leave people with nothing to remember. Decide what matters most in this moment.

04 / Message
Craft

Know what the format needs.

A campaign image, a product page and a creator post have different jobs. The idea should survive the change in format.

05 / Execution
Judgment

Know when to move on.

Keep asking what the work is teaching you. Adapt with purpose, and be willing to leave an execution behind.

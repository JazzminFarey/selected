## Source review

- **Google Drive primary source:** inventoried the root folder and all four Images subfolders plus Zip Material. Inspected all 38 image files (including duplicates), both MP4s and the original HTML benchmark. The two portfolio decks were read for source context; no deck pages, recreated diagrams or confidential material were used. Copy/brief documents were not used as creative. The standalone partnership slide is outside the approved projects.
- **New from Drive:** 17 originals selected: all seven `1000131097.png`–`1000131103.png` campaign images; Money Your Way 1/2/3 portrait/product JPGs; Flex `Ad 1`, `Ad 2`, `Ad 3`, `Performance Max 1`, and the `IMG-20260304-WA0050.jpg` master-of-your-money portrait; both MP4s. All files are mapped individually below. The new Money Your Way MP4 is in the reel and case page; the second Zip Plus product MP4 is on the Flex case page.
- **Taylor Aranki:** inspected the live complete case page, portrait gallery previews and the six-execution outdoor montage. Added the montage as a browser capture, with outer browser margins removed, because the download mechanism failed. The montage and four intact execution panels appear in the reel. Two embedded Vimeo players restricted access; their motion was not reviewed or used. No claim that all Taylor motion was inspected.
- **Robert Pregardt-Paur:** inspected his campaign post and the full existing ten-second multi-format motion compilation. Re-edited landscape, square and portrait panels, animated type and phone reveal; no separate new Robert source file acquired. His post describes 16 deliverables but not all were independently available. Other-client work on his public site was excluded.
- **Original Squarespace:** inspected saved full case pages and complete local images for allHearts, Barnardos Buddies, Jump Rope/Direct Mail and Walk Your Way. Live page retrieval failed. No separate Barnardos case was found. Retained genuine assets with new edit/composition; no new earlier-project downloads. Related, unapproved projects below the cases were excluded.

## Editorial decisions

The reel opens on actual animated Money Your Way creative, moves between portraits and bold Flex typography, pauses on product photography, then builds through outdoor executions into the Flex phone reveal. Shot lengths range from 0.5 to 2.6 seconds. Each project has unequal airtime. The last eight seconds concentrate on Zip rather than recapping every project. Portraits and squares retain the complete artwork; the only panel crops isolate intact executions from Robert/Taylor source montages. No black letterboxing, AI campaign images or separate end card.

The homepage uses the Drive `1000131098.png` Money Your Way portrait and the deliberately selected Flex woman/low-angle portrait. Finding Winners Faster remains typographic. Existing positioning, role descriptions, navigation and project list are retained. Footer uses the supplied wording.

User-authorized creative review assumes portfolio reuse permissions. Credits/public access do not establish permission. Final rights/provenance gate remains deferred until creative approval.
